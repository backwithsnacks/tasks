      How it works

      • Daily tasks untick themselves after 12 midnight.
      • Tap 🔒 on a daily task to keep it as it is tonight. It skips one reset, then goes back to normal.
      • 🔥 is your streak: how many days in a row you finished that task.
      • To-do tasks stay until you tick them. Ticked ones get cleared away after midnight.
      • Nothing ever leaves your phone. The app has no internet features at all.
